About the company

A small landlord company, run by its owner

XD PROPERTY RENTAL LTD was incorporated on 13 July 2020 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 12736577. The company is based in Barking, East London.

Its business is straightforward: it lets residential property that it owns itself. Companies House records its activity as SIC 68209, “Other letting and operating of own or leased real estate”.

XD Property Rental is a landlord, not a letting agency. Because the company lets its own homes, tenants deal directly with the owner, the party that actually makes decisions about the property, rather than with a third party acting on someone else’s behalf.

What we do

Renting out our own homes, and looking after them

Everything the company does relates to the residential property it owns. That keeps the arrangement simple for tenants: one landlord, one point of contact.

  • Homes to rent from the owner

    The company lets residential property that it owns. There is no agency between you and the landlord, so questions about the home go straight to the people responsible for it.

  • Clear tenancy set-up

    One point of contact from your first enquiry through to move-in. Tenancy terms are set by the owner, so the answers you get come from the people who decide them.

  • Repairs and upkeep

    As the landlord, the company looks after its own property during the tenancy, arranging repairs and maintenance for the homes it lets.

  • Rent and day-to-day matters

    Rent collection and everyday tenancy questions are handled by the landlord company directly, so you always know who you are dealing with.

XD Property Rental Ltd lets its own property only. It does not offer letting or management services.
